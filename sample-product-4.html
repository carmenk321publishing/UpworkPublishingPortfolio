Grade 4 Informational Reading Assessment: Learning to Prepare Food

Informational Reading Assessment · Grade 4 · RI.4.2

The Problem

Fourth-grade readers often struggle to separate a text's main idea from interesting but minor details, and to explain how key details support it. This 20–25-minute assessment uses an original informational passage with paragraph and line labels. It asks eight selected-response questions aligned to RI.4.2: main idea, supporting details, and summarizing.

Highlights

  • RI.4.2 alignment
  • Original informational passage
  • Item writing
  • Distractor rationales
  • Answer key and scoring notes

Learning Objectives

  • Determine the main idea of an informational text.
  • Identify key details that support the main idea.
  • Select relevant details for a summary.

Deliverables

  • Student Assessment (3 pgs, PDF): directions, labeled reading passage, and eight selected-response questions
  • Teacher Version (6 pgs, PDF): at-a-glance page, assessment design notes, item analysis with distractor rationales, answer key, scoring guidance, and credits

Tools Used

  • Perplexity AI (passage drafting and editorial feedback)
  • Microsoft Word and PDF export
  • Canva
