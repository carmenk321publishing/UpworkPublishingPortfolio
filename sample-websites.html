Instructional design · Interactive simulation

Build-a-Burrow: An Interactive ADDIE Simulation

ADDIE is the five-phase process instructional designers use to build learning. Build-a-Burrow turns it into practice: new designers work one real course idea through every phase, building a hillside burrow as they go.

  1. AnalyzeFind the real problem
  2. DesignSet the goal and shape
  3. DevelopBuild it for everyone
  4. ImplementLaunch it properly
  5. EvaluateCheck it worked
  • Audience: new and transitioning instructional designers
  • Feedback: flags unobservable objectives, missing root causes, and evaluation that drifts from the goal
  • Outcome: a downloadable course design outline of their own
  • Built in: highlight-to-define glossary, saved progress, keyboard and screen reader support

Learning design · Course-aware planning tool

CLIL Lesson Lab

Content and Language Integrated Learning asks teachers to plan a subject and a language at once. CLIL Lesson Lab turns that into a repeatable workflow: set a course up once, then build lessons that carry forward how the last one actually went.

  1. ObjectivesContent and language, tagged to level
  2. Five stagesA full lesson shape, timed
  3. Language bankFrames, vocabulary, support
  4. MaterialsWorksheet, answer key, glossary
  5. AuditSources checked, gaps flagged
  • Audience: teachers new to CLIL, subject or language side
  • Grounding: subject content drawn from retrieved sources, with every claim carrying a citation and anything unconfirmed flagged rather than hidden
  • Outcome: a complete lesson package, exportable to Word and PowerPoint
  • Built in: worked examples with the audit left visible, and a 5 Cs check that reads any plan strand by strand
