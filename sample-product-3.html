What Luke Can Do–What Luke Did Yesterday

Decodable Reading–Phonics Lesson · Grades 1–2

The Problem

First- and second-grade learners often read "-ed" words as if they had one sound, but the ending is spoken three ways: /t/, /d/ and /əd/. This teacher-led, 45-minute phonics lesson connects each base verb to its past-tense form through a short decodable story about Luke. Students read, sort, hear and spell the endings in context.

Highlights

Learning Objectives

  • Read a base word and its "-ed" form.
  • Say whether "-ed" sounds like /t/, /d/ or /əd/ by listening to the last sound in the base word.
  • Spell a sentence that includes an "-ed" word.
